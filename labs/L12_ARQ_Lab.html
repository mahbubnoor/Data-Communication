    Why the window has a limit
    
    
    
    
    
    Largest safe window
    
    
    Go-Back-N: window ≤ 2m − 1. Selective Repeat: window ≤ 2m−1. Push the slider one past the limit and the receiver mistakes an old frame for a new one.
