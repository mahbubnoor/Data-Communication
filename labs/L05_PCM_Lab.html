Is the sampling rate high enough?

Nyquist rate 2f Frequency the receiver sees

Below the Nyquist rate the samples fit a slower, false wave: aliasing. Try f = 5 Hz with fs = 6, 9, 10 and 12.

One bit per sample

Small steps track slowly: on steep slopes the staircase falls behind (slope overload). Large steps jitter on flat parts (granular noise).

PCM bit rate and bandwidth

fs = 2 × fmax · bit rate = fs × n × channels · minimum bandwidth ≈ bit rate / 2 = n × fmax per channel.
