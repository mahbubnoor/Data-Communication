Circuit: setup + L/R + links × propagation. Packet: (N + links − 1) × packet time + links × propagation + waiting. Headers are ignored here.

Datagrams are independent: they can take different paths, see different waiting times and arrive out of order. The transport layer at X puts them back in order.

A frame crosses three switches; each swaps the VCI using its table

Follow the VCI

A VCI is only meaningful on one link. Each switch looks up (incoming port, incoming VCI) and sends the frame out on a new port with a new VCI. The values match Forouzan's Figures 8.12–8.15.
