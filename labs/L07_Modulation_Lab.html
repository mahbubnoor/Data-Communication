Wi-Fi does exactly this: close to the router it uses many points (more bits per symbol); far away, where SNR is low, it falls back to fewer.

4-QAM labels follow Forouzan 5e (11 at 45°, Gray coded). 8-QAM follows the classic 2-amplitude, 4-phase constellation: first two bits pick the phase, the last bit the amplitude.
