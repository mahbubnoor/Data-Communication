A perfect square wave needs infinitely many harmonics, so its exact bandwidth is infinite. In practice we keep the few that carry most of the shape: the significant bandwidth.

Does the channel have enough bandwidth?

Minimum B ≈ N/2 Bits sent Bits received Bit errors

Lower B until the received bits start to break, then raise N. Faster bits need a wider channel.
